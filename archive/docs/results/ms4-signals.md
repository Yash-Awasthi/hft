# MS4: features and signals

Features: the C++ streaming engine (`src/strategy/features.hpp`, `multi_features.hpp`),
exported through the `hftpy` module; targets: the separate labeler
(`src/strategy/labeler.hpp`). Train 2025-12-08 to 2025-12-10, validation 2025-12-11; test
days untouched. Universe: `configs/universe.toml` (25 large-tick, 25 small-tick, SPY and
QQQ as cross-asset inputs), chosen from train days only. Produced by
`research/build_dataset.py` and `research/signals.py`; the run is logged in the registry
(`research_runs`, question Q1-signals).

Checks behind these numbers:
- Leakage: features up to t are bit-identical when events after t are deleted, shuffled or
  replaced (`Features.UnchangedWhenTheFutureIsPerturbed`); online RLS predictions do not
  move when unresolved targets change (`research/tests/test_estimators.py`).
- Batch export equals the scheduler-driven stream bit for bit
  (`research/tests/check_batch_streaming.py`, run in CI).
- Labeler against brute force (`Labeler.EventAndClockHorizonsAgainstBruteForce`).

Caveats:
- One validation day: intervals are across symbols (the stock is the unit), not days; they
  understate day-to-day variation.
- ITCH covers Nasdaq only, so every feature sees a partial book.
- Targets: `y_eN` is the mid change in ticks after N messages of the symbol, `y_Nms` after N
  milliseconds. Each symbol is sampled every k-th event so it contributes about 100k rows a
  day; train rows are further thinned to every 6th for the model fits (memory limit N4).
- ITCH sets the side of every hidden-execution (P) message to "B" (checked: 12,554 of
  12,554 for QQQ on 2025-12-11), so hidden-flow direction is inferred from the print
  against the mid.
- Tradable IC uses half the spread plus a $0.0030 taker fee as the threshold; the tradable
  share is the fraction of validation rows whose prediction clears it.
- RLS ranks well but its predictions are mis-scaled (negative R2); its forgetting factor and
  prior were not tuned, to keep validation out of model choices.

Train: ['2025-12-08', '2025-12-09', '2025-12-10'], 2774678 rows. Validation: ['2025-12-11'], 5469190 rows, 50 symbols.
